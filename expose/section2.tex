\section{Objective and Research Interest}
This research investigates image focus detection based on a segmentation and
classification framework. The main focus is to compare traditional machine
learning methods and lightweight convolutional neural networks under conditions
of limited training data.

Convolutional neural networks are widely recognized for their ability to
automatically learn discriminative features, but they typically require a large
number of labeled samples. Traditional machine learning approaches, in
contrast, rely on handcrafted features and simpler models, which may be more
suitable for small datasets and fast training. Since lightweight CNN
architectures contain a limited number of parameters, it remains unclear
whether they can consistently outperform classical methods in low-sample
scenarios.

The objective of this study is to empirically evaluate and compare both
approaches in terms of classification performance and efficiency when
distinguishing focused and unfocused image regions. Through this comparative
analysis, the research aims to identify under which conditions traditional
machine learning methods are sufficient and when lightweight neural networks
provide observable advantages for image focus detection.
